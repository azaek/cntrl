Cntrl Console is a web app for the machines you run: servers, VPSes, a Raspberry Pi, the Mac mini in the closet. Each machine runs the cntrl agent, which is open source, and Console shows them all, live, at [console.cntrl.pw](https://console.cntrl.pw).

## How it works

The agent opens one encrypted connection out to Console, the way a browser opens a website. Nothing on the machine listens on a network port, so there's no port to forward and no VPN to run, behind NAT or not.

The machine decides what Console may do. A new machine only reports: its readings, processes, services, containers, network and logs. To let Console restart a service or power the machine off, you allow it on the machine itself. See [Permissions](/docs/console/permissions).

## What you can do

- Watch CPU, memory, disks, network and GPUs live, with charts that go back as far as the machine keeps them, up to a year.
- Look at its processes, services, containers, disks, listening ports and logs as they're written.
- Restart a service or container, stop a process, or restart or shut down the machine, where its permissions allow.
- Get alerts by email, Discord, Slack, ntfy or webhook when a machine goes offline, a reading stays over a line, or a service stops.
- Check websites, ports and cron jobs, from cntrl's network or from one of your machines.
- Invite people to your organization, as admins or members.

## Runs on

- Linux with systemd, on x86_64 or aarch64
- macOS, on Apple silicon or Intel
- Windows 10, 11 or Server, on x64 or ARM64, in beta

## Plans

Free covers 3 devices and 2 checks for one person, with alerts by email. Pro and Team add devices, people, every alert channel and checks every 30 seconds. See [People and plans](/docs/console/people#plans) for the details, and [console.cntrl.pw](https://console.cntrl.pw/#pricing) for prices.
