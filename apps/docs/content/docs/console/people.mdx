Devices, checks and alerts belong to an organization. **Organization** has its name, its people, its invitations and its plan.

## Roles

| Role   | Can                                                                                                                        |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Member | See devices, their stats, services, processes and logs, and change nothing                                                 |
| Admin  | Act on devices where their policy allows, add and remove them, make checks and alerts, and invite people and change their roles |
| Owner  | Everything an admin can. Manages and pays for the organization's plan, and can hand the organization to someone else      |

An organization has exactly one owner. Ownership moves only when the owner hands it on, confirmed with a code sent to their email.

A role never goes past a machine's [permissions](/docs/console/permissions). An admin can restart a service only where the machine allows `services.manage`.

## Invite people

Owners and admins invite people by email, with a role. An invitation lasts a week. The person signs in with the address it went to, and can accept or decline it. If Console couldn't send the email, it says so, and you can copy the link and send it yourself.

Removing someone ends their access to the organization's devices at once. They keep their account, and anyone can invite them back.

## Plans

|                     | Free                   | Pro                 | Team                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Devices             | 3                      | 25                  | 100                 |
| People              | Just you               | 3                   | 25                  |
| Alert rules         | 1, by email            | As many as you like | As many as you like |
| Alerts sent a month | 30                     | No limit            | No limit            |
| Alert history       | 7 days                 | 90 days             | 90 days             |
| Checks              | 2, every 5 or 15 min   | 20, from every 30 s | 50, from every 30 s |
| Heartbeats          | No                     | Yes                 | Yes                 |
| Live readings       | Pause after 30 min idle | Stay live          | Stay live           |

Each organization has its own plan, which its owner manages, and the limits above are each organization's own. You can own one Free organization. Another one without a paid plan is paused: its devices are off and no one can use it until it has one.

Every tab and action, the charts, and each machine's policy, pause and audit log are the same on every plan.

Prices are in US dollars on [console.cntrl.pw](https://console.cntrl.pw/#pricing). You can upgrade or cancel from Console at any time.

### Past a plan's limits

After a downgrade, an organization can have more than its new plan covers:

- **People**: the owner chooses who stays, and the rest are removed.
- **Devices**: the owner chooses which to keep. The rest stay listed but disabled: they can't be opened, show nothing and don't alert, until a plan covers them again.
- **Checks, rules and channels**: the ones past the plan keep running, and can be deleted, but not changed or paused.

## Delete an organization or your account

Both are at the bottom of **Organization**. Console emails you a code to confirm, and with it the deletion happens at once. It can't be undone.

- **An organization** can be deleted by its owner. Its devices are disconnected, and its checks, alert rules, channels and alert history are deleted. Everyone in it loses access. The agents stay installed until you run `sudo cntrl uninstall` on each machine.
- **Your account** can be deleted once no organization you own has anyone else in it: transfer each one, or remove everyone else, first. Organizations you own alone are deleted with it, and you leave every organization you're in.

A paid plan ends at once when its organization is deleted, and the rest of the period isn't refunded automatically. Write to [support@cntrl.pw](mailto:support@cntrl.pw) if you'd like it back.
